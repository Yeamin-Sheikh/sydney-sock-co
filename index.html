Ethical Provenance

From sunlit pastures to circular knitting

We trace every spool of yarn directly to certified non-mulesed Merino sheep farms across regional New South Wales. The raw wool is scoured in closed-loop systems, spun to fine 19.5-micron threads, and knit on 200-needle Italian machinery for an imperceptible toe seam.

Community Purpose

Socks are the most requested item in Sydney homeless shelters

When we launched Sydney Sock Co in Surry Hills, we discovered that fresh, clean socks and warm meals are rarely donated to community missions. We partnered directly with local Sydney outreach hubs to establish a strict 1-for-1 giveback promise.

100-day comfort guarantee and free returns

We back our ethical Australian manufacturing with generous trial and warranty terms.

100-day wear test guarantee

Wear your first pair for up to 100 days. If you do not agree they are the most comfortable socks in your drawer, send them back for a full refund or size exchange.

Hole-free 12-month replacement

If any pair develops a wear hole within 12 months of purchase, simply email our Surry Hills workshop with a photo and we will send a brand new replacement free of charge.

Free Australian return shipping

Every Australian order includes a prepaid Australia Post return satchel. Simply drop it into any red post box for seamless returns.
